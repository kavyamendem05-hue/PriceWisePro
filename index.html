GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PriceWise?

PriceWise is a product price comparison website that helps users explore products and compare prices.

Is PriceWise free to use?

Yes. This student project demonstration is free to explore.

Are the displayed prices live?

No. The current catalogue uses sample prices for demonstration. Live prices require authorized retailer integrations.

How can I compare products?

Search for a product and select the Compare Prices button to view its comparison table.

Does PriceWise sell products?

No. PriceWise is a comparison demonstration. Purchases would take place on the retailer's website.
